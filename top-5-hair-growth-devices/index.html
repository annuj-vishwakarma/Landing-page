What You Should Look For

  • Red light and near-infrared wavelengths are widely studied for improving scalp circulation and stimulating hair follicles.
  • Broad and balanced coverage helps support more consistent results and avoids patchy treatment.
  • A device that feels comfortable and uses skin-safe, medical-grade materials is easier to use regularly.
  • Flexible settings make it easier to tailor treatments for sensitive scalps or different routines.

What You Should Avoid

  • Hard plastic or non-medical materials can feel uncomfortable and may irritate the scalp during longer sessions.
  • If the device does not clearly explain its light technology, it is harder to judge its effectiveness.
  • A poor fit can make daily use difficult and may reduce the amount of light reaching the scalp.
  • Devices that feel fiddly or confusing are less likely to be used consistently over time.
